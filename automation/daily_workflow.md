# Phase 5: Automation & Daily Workflow

To hit your ₹500/week goal with only 1-2 hours of work, follow this "Low-Touch" automation system.

## 🤖 1. Content Generation (The AI Engine)
**Prompt for ChatGPT (Run every Monday):**
> "I run a smart timetable generator for students. Generate 7 catchy titles for Pinterest and 3 ideas for Instagram Reels that focus on 'Exam Stress' and 'Time Management'. Make them sound relatable to high school students."

**Goal:** Get your weekly content ideas in 5 minutes.

---

## 🎨 2. Design Automation (Canva)
- Use **Canva Bulk Create**:
  1. Create one template for a "Topper Tip".
  2. Upload a CSV with 10 tips from ChatGPT.
  3. Canva will auto-generate 10 posts.
- **Time spent:** 20 minutes.

---

## 🗓️ 3. Scheduling (Set & Forget)
- Use **Buffer** or **Meta Business Suite** (Free):
  - Schedule all 7 Pinterest pins on Monday.
  - Schedule 3 Instagram Reels for Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday.
- **Time spent:** 30 minutes.

---

## 🛠️ 4. Daily Monitoring (The "15-Minute" Rule)
Spend 15 minutes a day doing ONLY this:
1. Check Gumroad for sales.
2. Reply to comments on Pinterest/Instagram (Engagement = more traffic).
3. If a post goes viral, link it directly to the **Premium Topper Pack**.

---

## 📈 5. Growth Hack: The GitHub Loop
Keep your GitHub repository active. 
- **Weekly Task:** Add a tiny "styling" update or a new small feature (like a 'Break Timer') and push to GitHub. 
- **Result:** GitHub's algorithm keeps your project at the top of "Recently Updated" for relevant tags, bringing in free developer traffic.
