**Keywords:** study smarter not harder, time management for students, exam preparation tips, smart study planner.

---

We’ve all seen the YouTube videos: "Study 12 hours a day with me."

While they look inspiring, they are often the fastest way to **burnout and bad grades.** Studying is not about the *number of hours*; it's about the *quality of focus*.

### The Myth of the 12-Hour Study Day
Your brain has a limited capacity for "Deep Work." After 4-5 hours of intense focus, your retention rate drops by nearly 60%. If you keep pushing, you aren't learning—you're just sitting in a chair.

### The Fix: The "Smart Allocation" Strategy
Instead of trying to do everything every day, you need to prioritize.
1. **Identify the 'Heavy Hitters':** Which subjects have the most weight in your final grade?
2. **Assign a Difficulty Score:** Scale of 1 to 5.
3. **Automate the Math:** Stop spending your precious brainpower deciding *what* to study. Use a tool like the [Smart Timetable Generator](LINK_TO_YOUR_TOOL) to do the scheduling for you.
